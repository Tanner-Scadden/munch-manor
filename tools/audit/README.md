# Audit tools

Scripts that drive the game in headless Chrome to take screenshots and measure frame time. They live outside `src/` and keep their own `package.json`, so `playwright-core` never becomes a dependency of the game.

## Setup

```sh
cd tools/audit
bun install
```

They use the local Google Chrome or the cached Playwright headless shell.

The harness boots the game's own `init`, `update`, and `view` at a state chosen by query string, such as `?wave=8&start=wave&gun=smg&fill&crowd=30`. It is test tooling only and never ships. `harness/config.ts` decodes the query string once through the `HarnessConfig` Schema, with the defaults below, so an unknown key or a bad value (a typo, `wave=abc`, `gun=uzi`, `seen=Bob`) stops the page with the Schema error on screen instead of quietly showing the default scene. `harness/entry.ts` is the page and its scene switching, `harness/scenario.ts` and `harness/room.ts` build a scene's Model, `harness/sceneUpdate.ts` wraps the game's update for the scene, and `harness/settings.ts` holds what the page keeps across scenes. A flag such as `fill` is on when present, with any value or none.

- **The run.** `wave` picks the wave (0, the default, is the home menu), `start=wave` starts it instead of its break, and `mode=endless` starts an endless run. `phase=gameover` or `phase=victory` with `stats` fills the run stats panel, and `phase=paused` pauses the round. `elapsed` starts a wave's clock that many ms in, so the WAVE banner is gone. `prep` sets the break's remaining ms (default 20000), and `break=open` makes it the untimed first break, `breakMs` ms in. `money`, `barricades`, and `turrets` fill the wallet and the trap stock. `device=touch` shows touch wording, and `py` with `px` places the player (`px` alone does nothing, and without `px` he keeps his x). The staged zombies below stand relative to `px`,`py`, which default to 160,90 for them.
- **Guns.** `gun=smg` or `gun=shotgun` gives a spare gun, with `rounds` total rounds, `loaded` in the magazine (the SMG's, default 30), and `tier` its upgrade tier. `pistol` sets the pistol's tier. `aim=x,y` holds the trigger down aimed at that arena point, and `god` keeps the player at full health.
- **The room.** `fill` lays the most decals, `crowd=N` stands N zombies around the room, and `tough` gives them 40 times their health so the room stays full. `gasroom=N` fills the crowd with N Gasbags and walks new ones in at the doors whenever shots and chain bursts pop them. `lineup=wear` stands every kind at every wear stage in rows, `lineup=boss` the boss at each of its five stages, `lineup=sizes` every kind at full health in a row, and `lineup=armor` a Big Lug at each armor stage with the shed pieces launched.
- **New zombies.** `seen` lists the kinds already introduced this session, such as `seen=Shambler,Runner` or `seen=all`. With `start=wave` it also raises the new zombie card for the first kind in the wave that is not on the list, since the card only comes up when a wave starts, and `card` sets the card's age in ms.
- **Power-ups.** `pickups` lays pickups in a row on the floor, such as `pickups=all` or `pickups=Heart,Pierce`, at `pickupY` (default 128) and `pickupAge` ms old (default 2000, so 8300 shows the despawn blink). `boosts` starts boosts, such as `boosts=RapidFire,Damage`, the first with `boostMs` left (default 5000) and each next one 2.2 seconds less, so the last can sit in its expiry blink. `callout=Pierce` puts that power-up's name callout over the player, `calloutAge` ms old (default 400), explained with its blurb or, with `calloutLook=Named`, the repeat grab's icon and name only. `seenpowerups=RapidFire,Pierce` (or `all`) marks kinds as grabbed before, so a pickup under the player (`pickups=RapidFire&px=160&py=128`) shows the repeat callout when grabbed; `callout=Burp` with a small age also shows the burp ring. `bounty` floats three money popups (two kill bounties and a wave bonus) across the middle of the room.
- **The boss.** `boss=arriving|stomp|belch|charge|rush|spit|dazed|fighting` puts the boss in that state: `arriving` rattles `door` (default East) with `arriveMs` left, `stomp` and `belch` start that wind-up with `windMs` left, `charge` braces toward `tx`,`ty` with `windMs` left, `rush` is mid-rush toward `tx`,`ty`, `spit` has a glob in the air toward `tx`,`ty` with `globMs` left, `dazed` is stunned for `windMs`, and `fighting` stalks with `cooldownMs` left. The boss stands at `bx`,`by` with `bosshp` of its health (a share, default 1). `enraged` makes him enraged, and `puddles=x,y;x,y` lays puddles with `puddleMs` left. On wave 8 without `boss`, the boss is planned as the game plans it, so the door preview shows its marker.
- **The other kinds.** `gasbags` stands an idle Gasbag, one shot twice beside the player, and one fusing with `fuseMs` left. `frenzy` stands a frenzied pack and a fading straggler with a HORDE! callout `hordeAge` ms old, and `wipe` adds a pack bonus popup. `chucker=heft|throw|taunt|shield|dazed` stages a Big Leaguer at `cx`,`cy`: hefting a Groaner with `heftMs` left, a Groaner in flight with `flyMs` left, right after a throw, holding a Groaner toward the player as a shield with `shieldMs` left, or a dazed Groaner. `crawler=surfacing|cling` stages an Ankle Biter at `wx`,`wy` with `surfMs` left, or one on the player's ankles.
- **Traps and difficulty.** `planks=West,North` boards those doors with `plankhp`, and `chewers=N` stands Groaners (a Big Lug as the fifth) by the West door. `doorway` stands zombies mid-entry at every door, hurt so the Big Lug's bar shows, with one in the kitchen's doorway when `openexits=Kitchen` opens it. `ask` shows the first-time difficulty card, and `difficulty` and `nightmare` set the setting as in the Model, such as `difficulty=Hard&nightmare=Unlocked`.
- **Lessons and runs.** `learned` marks lessons learned, such as `learned=move,fire,startWave`, so a later tutorial hint shows. `traphint=Barricade` or `traphint=Turret` raises that trap hint with the kind selected (give it stock with `barricades` or `turrets`). `runs` sets the runs played in the bests, which decides whether the end screens ask for feedback.
- **The home menu.** `best` sets the best endless wave this session, and `bests` gives a session with a best endless wave, a fastest win, and kills. `kills=Shambler:40,Boss:2` sets the kills per kind in the bests, `opened=Shambler,Tank` the Munchopedia entries opened, `newentries=Runner` the NEW ENTRY pop, `homemode=endless` the mode, and `newsseen` marks the newest What's New day seen. `newer` fakes a newer live build (its value, or `next`), `fit=home` or `fit=full` fakes the full screen check, `support` sets a test tip jar link so the coffee button shows, and `rotate` fakes a phone that can lock how it turns, so Settings shows ROTATE.
- **The Profile.** `profile=look|name|account|settings` opens the Profile on that tab, `name` sets the display name, `sync=local|connecting|synced|offline|broken` with `devices=N` fakes the sync status, and `link=requesting|showing|entering|redeeming|linked|failed` a step of linking.
- **The look.** `look=Body,Hair,HairColor,Skin,Shirt,Outfit,Print,Beard,Glasses` sets the character's look, such as `look=Slim,Ponytail,Red,Tan,Teal,Hoodie,Rainbow,,Glasses` (an empty or missing part keeps the default; `Beard` and `Glasses` turn those on), `picker` opens the Profile on Look on the screen it starts on, `looksection=Body|Hair|Outfit` picks the Look tab's little tab, and `looksheet` swaps the game for a sheet of every option of every part on both bodies, every outfit in every print, and the beard and glasses, plus walk frames, both facings, and the hit flash for four mixed looks. `shotsLooks.ts` shoots the whole sheet on d1280, l844, and p390.
- **Co-op.** `coop=` stages a co-op screen with a network that never answers: `tile` (home with the CO-OP bar open), `hub`, `problem`, `code`, `asking`, `queue`, `offer`, `accepted`, `lobby`, `host` (four seats, one READY), `ready`, `result`, `arena`, `away`, `ended`, or the end card with the crew board: `card` (four players, Game Over), `cardwon` (three, a win), or `cardduo` (two). `live` is the one that moves: the page joins a real room playing in a Web Worker with `seats` seats (default 4) and `crowd` zombies that never die (default 40), its other seats scripted players (see the frame time gate below). Without it, home shows the bar padlocked.
- **Measuring.** `freeze=N` runs exactly N frames of a fixed 1/60 second with a fixed seed and then holds the picture, so two builds can be compared pixel for pixel. It counts frames only once the surface is measured, and sets `window.__isFrozen` when it holds. `tick=N` plays the same N fixed frames at once, before the first render, and holds the picture there, so a scene 1.2 s in (`tick=72`) is ready at once instead of after 1.2 s; the Commands those frames give, such as opening the shop, run once the scene is in. `seed` sets the seed without freezing. `instrument` records each frame's `update` and `view` time in `window.__timings`, and `nodev` leaves out the Foldkit DevTools badge. `director` is the video tool's mode (`tools/video/README.md`, `harness/director.ts`): the page never ticks by itself, `window.__director.step(n)` plays n fixed ticks with the shot's scripted inputs, and the sounds and music cues the game asks for are logged by tick instead of played.

### The ready signal and switching scenes

- **Ready.** The harness sets `window.__harnessReady` (and `data-harness-ready` on the root element) once the scene has rendered with the fonts loaded and every image the page created decoded, the boot has read the save, and, with `freeze`, once the frozen frame is reached. `harness/readiness.ts` keeps every image element the page makes, however the game loads its images, so the signal does not depend on how sprites are built. Scripts wait for it with `waitForReady` (or `gotoScene`) instead of sleeping. `window.__whenSettled()` (`whenSettled` in `lib.ts`) does the same after a click or a key.
- **Animations.** `shot` takes screenshots with Playwright's `animations: 'disabled'`, so an animation that ends is shown at its end and a loop at its start. `settleAnimations` jumps every animation that ends to its end instead of waiting for it, letting the page settle around each pass (a tap often starts its animation a Command later), for checks that measure a button's box and before the next tap.
- **Switching in place.** `window.__loadScene(query)` decodes another query and swaps its scene in without a reload: it stops the runtime, as a page leaving would, and starts it again on the new scene (the store and the other services are built once for the page and shared). It builds the Model from the query as init does, keeps the measured surface, counts the save as read and empty (as a fresh browser context would), runs init's other Commands again (the seed and the device, screen fit, and orientation lock checks), releases what open dialogs hold (the inert background, focus trap, and scroll lock), and resets focus, full screen, and scroll. A bad query sets `window.__sceneError` instead of a new scene. Page-level keys such as `nodev` stay as the page was opened. `scenePages` in `lib.ts` keeps one page per screen and worker and switches scenes on it (`loadScene`), opening a new browser context only for the first scene on a screen or a scene marked `fresh`, such as the iPhone tip in `taps.ts`. `FRESH_PAGES=1` opens every scene in a new page, as before, to check that switching changes no picture.

### The built harness

Every harness script calls `useHarness` first. It hashes everything the harness build reads (`src/`, `tools/audit/harness/`, `tools/sprites/`, `public/`, `patches/`, `package.json`, `bun.lock`, and `tsconfig.json`), builds the harness with Vite into `munch-manor-harness/<hash>` in the temp directory when that folder is missing (about 2 s), and serves it with a small Bun static server on a free port of the script's own, so scripts in several worktrees never fight over a port. The folder is named by content, so every worktree on the same code shares one build, and only the newest eight builds are kept. A built page loads in 6 requests instead of the dev server's 250 and 22 MB, and has no DevTools badge.

- `HARNESS_DEV=1` uses the dev server on 4174 instead, for live editing. Start it yourself (see Servers).
- `BASE=<url>` points every script at a harness you started, building nothing.

Measure frame time on a built harness, not the dev server on 4174. The dev server serves unbundled modules with development checks and the Foldkit devtools, and a frame there costs two to three times what it costs in the production build.

## Scripts

Run every heavy script here through the heavy guard from the repo root, never directly: `bun run heavy -- bun tools/audit/perf.ts`, `bun run heavy -- bun tools/audit/profile.ts`, or `TAG=after bun run heavy -- bun tools/audit/shots17.ts`. The guard runs the timing scripts (`perf.ts`, `perf-check.ts`, `profile.ts`, `measure.ts`, `splash.ts`) alone, so frame times are not skewed by a balance run in another worktree, and runs up to four screenshot and functional audits (the shots scripts, `taps.ts`, and the like) at once within its load budget, each costing about one core per browser context. `taps.ts`, `closes.ts`, `shots34.ts`, and `shots33.ts` also run up to three scenes or screens at once in their own browser contexts (`TAPS_PARALLEL`, `SHOTS_PARALLEL`, 1 for one after another), with the report in the usual order. See `tools/heavy/README.md`. The scripts find their files from their own folder, so they work from the root. The commands below are shown by script name. The shared pieces live in `lib.ts`: the browser, the screens (`DESKTOP_1280`, `PHONE_667`, `UPRIGHT_390`, and the rest) and `openPage`, the `TAG`, `BASE`, `ONLY`, and `VIEWPORTS` variables, `useHarness`, `harnessUrl`, the ready signal (`gotoScene`, `waitForReady`, `whenSettled`), `scenePages` and `loadScene`, the pixel compare, and `finish`, which prints the problems and exits with 1.

- `bun run heavy -- bun tools/audit/perf.ts` records `requestAnimationFrame` deltas for the idle, busy, and worst campaign scenarios, two endless ones, `endless15` (endless wave 15) and `endlessCap` (the room held at the alive cap), `gasbags` (wave 6 with 16 Gasbags kept in the room while the bot pops them), `kitchen` (the worst room with the kitchen open and the player by its ovens), and `coopArena` (the co-op arena staged with three players and a network that never answers). ## Frame time gate

`bun run perf:check` waits for the heavy guard, then builds the harness for production, serves it on port 4179 (it refuses to start if the port is taken), and plays the `worst`, `endlessCap`, and `coopLive` scenarios at 1x, 4x, and 6x CPU throttle in headless Chrome, best of two trials each. `coopLive` is a co-op client in a moving room: `?coop=live&seats=4&crowd=40` runs a real room in a Web Worker (`harness/liveRoom.worker.ts`, its World from `harness/liveRoom.ts`), three of its four seats scripted players with Sessions of their own and the page's seat the bot, over the real wire with 40 ms each way (`harness/liveNet.ts`), 40 zombies that never die. It compares the results to `frame-baseline.json` and exits with 1 when a gated number regresses past its tolerance.

| Number                       | What it is                                                                   | Gate                                           |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| js ms                        | The game's own `update` plus `view` per frame                                | Always. 25% over                               |
| main thread ms per frame     | All renderer main thread work in the window, painting included               | Always. 25% over                               |
| fps                          | Frames per second from `requestAnimationFrame`                               | `GATE=all`. 15% under                          |
| p95 frame ms                 | The 95th percentile `requestAnimationFrame` delta                            | `GATE=all`. 30% over                           |
| share of ticked frames drawn | Of the animation frames with a frame tick, the share that drew in that frame | At 1x, always. Under 0.9 (`DRAWN_SHARE_FLOOR`) |
| drawn fps                    | Animation frames per second that drew                                        | `GATE=all`. 15% under                          |

The two CPU time numbers are the primary gate. Frame deltas swing with how busy the host is, and on a fast machine the game holds the display's refresh rate until it is far over budget, so FPS hides a regression that CPU time shows. The CPU time numbers still slow down on a loaded host, so the check first times a fixed JavaScript workload and scales the CPU limits by how much slower it ran than when the baseline was recorded. Set `GATE=cpu` on a busy machine to report FPS and p95 without failing on them.

- `UPDATE=1 bun run perf:check` records a new baseline. Record it on an idle machine after a deliberate change in cost, and commit `frame-baseline.json`. With `SCENARIOS` it records only those scenarios' rows, each with its own calibration, and keeps the others.
- Each row also prints ticks per drawn frame. With `instrument` the harness records the animation frame (its `document.timeline` time) each view and each frame tick that changed the Model ran in (`window.__timings.viewFrames` and `tickFrames`); a render a frame late shows as 2 ticks per draw and half the ticked frames drawn.
- `SCENARIOS`, `RATES`, `SECONDS`, and `TRIALS` change what runs. `JS_TOLERANCE`, `BUSY_TOLERANCE`, `FPS_TOLERANCE`, and `P95_TOLERANCE` take fractions such as `0.25`.
- `BASE=http://localhost:4177/` measures a server you already started instead of building one.

The deterministic half of the guard runs in `bun run test`: `src/perf.scene.test.ts` builds full rooms and counts the canvas shapes and paint operations a frame costs.
