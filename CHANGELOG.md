# What's new in Munch Manor

One entry per day, newest first. Only things a player would notice, a handful of bullets at most, written for players and not for us.

## November 12, 2026: Grab a controller

- 🎮 Plug in a controller and play a run with it: left stick walks, right stick aims, right trigger fires, Start pauses, and it rumbles when you get munched (turn that off under Settings, RUMBLE).
- 🛋️ Every menu works on the controller too: the d-pad or stick picks, A presses, B backs out, and the bumpers flip tabs. Couch to manor, no mouse needed.
- 🎯 Touch aim helps a bit less: it still nudges your shots onto a zombie, just more gently.
- 🤝 A co-op wipe goes straight to the score card, no lobby peeking in first. RUN IT BACK is now PLAY AGAIN.
- 🎒 Tap the dare list on your HUD (or press Tab) to pull the dare card back out: everything you've found sits in your satchel, each with a little story, and new finds plop right in.

## November 11, 2026: Catch your breath

- 🏃 Hotfoots catch their breath on Dare and Double Dare: still the quickest in the house, just not quite so quick.

## November 10, 2026: I dare you

Your friends dared you to go in. Heads up: a co-op run going when the update lands ends, so start a fresh one.

- 🎩 Your friends dared you into Munch Manor: find what's on the dare card (every room you unboard hides one), beat Uncle Chunk at the front door (he fights dirty, and his big gold key flies into the lock), then walk out together. ESCAPED! New here? Your first break walks you through it, and PLAY TUTORIAL on How to play replays it.
- 🗝️ Rattles closed his shop: zombies drop skeleton keys for padlocked boards (the first one's a cheap 8) and the fridge, guns wait in display cases you crack open with feats, guns level up as you splat (watch the bar under each one fill, then BEEFY!), the kitchen stove cooks a perk onto the gun in your hand, ammo never runs out (reloads still do), and 1, 2, and 3 grab a gun or your knife on a keyboard.
- 🕯️ Runs start at the dining room's table: shoot the chandelier onto the crowd, then find the parlour, a library with a secret shelf, and a pitch-black cellar where zombies stay invisible till they're breathing on you, shuffled into new spots every run. Rummage trunks, yank hollow books, haul up the dumbwaiter, and work a room's gizmo mid-wave (Groaners waltz, book stacks topple, barrels bowl, soup goes everywhere), but opening a room wakes its guests.
- 🚗 A spooky intro opens every run: the drive up the hill, a knock, a knock back, and SLAM. Tap to skip, but never the slam. Then the dare card pops out of your pocket: find these, then get out!
- 🧟 The house got dressed up: zombies are party guests and old daredevils, power-ups are the Wind-Up Key, Family Silver, Dancing Shoes, Hat Pins, and Dinner Gong, and the stakes are Dare, Double Dare, Triple Dare, and Triple Dog Dare. The Big Lug is a glutton in a stock pot with a frying pan shield, and the Kaboomer lugs a real round bomb.
- ⏱️ Breaks count down on every difficulty now (30 s on Dare and Double Dare), and READY early pays bonus rank. Crates, the pantry, and the stove stay open mid-wave, and traps mend mid-wave too (just slower). Waves hit harder and end sooner (meaner on Triple Dare, comfier on Dare), zombies pour in near wherever you're hiding, the last three stragglers hustle over with a red arrow if they're off screen, and the pause menu shows your dare list, keys, and rooms.

## November 8, 2026: Fat fingers welcome

- 👆 Taps land where you aim: the Profile's close button no longer steals taps from its tabs, and the shop guide's BACK is full size on phones.

## November 7, 2026: Skip to the munching

- 🤝 Co-op is open from your very first visit. Grab a friend's room link and jump straight in.
- ⏩ Played before? Tap SKIP IT on the first difficulty card and go straight into a real run, no tutorial.

## November 3, 2026: Now we're cooking

- 🔥 The kitchen is worth the trip: cook a perk into your gun at the stove (Spicy, Crispy, or Hearty), turn the gas valve to light the parlour fireplace and roast whatever walks past, and raid the pantry for a free goodie every break. Kills in the kitchen pay extra too.
- 🧊 The fridge plays fair: its odds are on the door, Duds are rare, a Sandwich gives a refund too, your first pull each break is cheaper, and a new weapon keeps your old gun's upgrades.
- 🥶 Brain Freeze bites now: it stings, reaches farther, has more charges, shatters harder and splashes, and can freeze a Big Lug solid. The Pressure Cooker charges way faster and never hurts you.
- 😇 Nightmare no longer has team damage. Want it? Turn on Friendly Fire in the lobby for bonus score.
- 🚑 Knocked down? You have 20 seconds now, and you can yell MEDIC! for help. Save a buddy in the nick of time for a CLUTCH!

## November 2, 2026: Something's cooking

- 🍲 The boarded kitchen door now glows, steams, and smells like soup, Rattles hollers about it every break until you open it, and a little house in your breaks counts the rooms you've found. Rattles also wrote a shop guide: tap the ? in his shop for bars, upgrades, and a fridge full of ???.
- 🎒 The co-op lobby got friendlier: tap your own name (it has a pencil) to rename yourself, dice and all, and buy a hero right there. Pings are WiFi icons now, KICK is a door with an arrow, and tapping a pick that's the host's says HOST PICKS.
- 👟 The Track Star's magazines are barely smaller now, and power-ups wait out the break: grab one late in a wave and its time carries into the next. Bot buddies quit tailing you: each one guards its own door, goes hunting, grabs power-ups, and no longer gets stuck in a corner. And knocked down in co-op or with bots, you can still stab from the floor with your knife or Clang Pan.
- 🎲 Run modifiers: pick Friendly Fire, Big Crowd, Broke, or Glass in the lobby to make a run meaner and pay up to +465% rank and Bones. Glass means one heart for real: no spare hearts, no fridge Sandwich, and revives get up on one. And Endless zombies keep beefing up, faster the deeper you go.
- 🧼 Menus got a tidy-up: every close and back button matches, corners and borders line up, the shop stops hopping between pages, and a new hero or hat on the end card says NOW AVAILABLE or NOW PURCHASABLE, then tucks itself away (tap to dismiss).
- 🕷️ Ankle Biters are zombie spiders now (quicker out of the floor, more of them, and easier to hit), and the Big Leaguer got a pitching coach: he brings three Groaners, runs to grab any he sees, throws quicker, lobs Hotfoots too, and whatever lands gets up angry and faster.

## November 1, 2026: Pick your hero

- 🎩 PLAY opens a lobby to pick your hero, mode, and difficulty before a run. Rank up to meet a hero (a hard hat, a sweatband, a visor, or a chef's toque, each with a trade-off), then spend Bones to take them home (try one on first). Tap + BOT to bring up to three bot buddies along. The home menu got tidied too: MULTIPLAYER says its name, MORE is bigger, and your bests badge opens the Munchopedia.
- 🎩 Spend your Bones on hats, shirts, prints, and gun skins in your Look tab (try one on before you buy, NEW badges on fresh unlocks), wear them on any hero, and rank up to be gifted a few for free: a party hat, a cowboy hat, a witch hat, even a traffic cone.
- 🎒 Your hero comes along in co-op: everyone in the room starts as the hero they picked, and the prop shows by their name in the lobby, where you can swap it too. Every hero now wears their trade, so you can tell who's who. A game update no longer drops a co-op run.
- 🪵 Planks and gnomes cost more but finally pull their weight, and the Handyman, Track Star, and Line Cook got a tune-up.
- 🧈 Buttery smooth: the game now draws every frame instead of every other one, so everything moves twice as smoothly, in co-op too. And breaks on Easy and Normal have no timer now: shop all you like, then smack the big gold READY, which glows when the whole crew is waiting on you. Co-op skips the new zombie cards too.
- ❤️ Crunchier health bars: zombies, Uncle Chunk, and bleeding teammates get outlined, chunked bars with a shiny top edge. On Hard and Nightmare, Uncle Chunk takes way more to drop, and attacks more often. And on Nightmare your buddies' bullets and lids no longer hurt you.

## October 31, 2026: Rattles has notes

- 🧭 New here? Your first breaks walk you through it: a free Plank Pile from Rattles, then a free kitchen and a free fridge roll, with the clock on hold. Confetti included.
- 🔫 Your gun now yells before it runs dry: the ammo blinks amber, then red, the last shots tick, and Rattles hollers from his stall, where one tap at his AMMO window buys you more mid-wave. Out of everything? Out comes the knife, or the Clang Pan, which now takes the knife's slot so you keep both guns. On Easy and Normal, your gun reloads itself the moment you ease off the trigger.
- 💀 Getting munched is a moment now: you topple, the zombies pile on, MUNCHED! slams down, and your run counts up. Go further or splat more than ever for a NEW BEST! Winning rains confetti (and a little gore) and crowns the MVP in co-op. Hit SHARE to brag anywhere: it opens your phone's share sheet, or copies your brag to paste. Every run fills a rank bar and pays Bones, with a fanfare when you rank up and a badge by your player on the home menu. And no more walling the room shut: planks board up half the doors at most.
- 🔍 Phones zoom in closer, so zombies are easier to spot, and your thumb's aim gets a little zombie magnet held sideways too. Held upright, your phone now shows more of the room, so fewer zombies sneak up from just off screen. Taps land where you mean them too: the settings X closes every time, buttons answer with another finger down, a mid-wave mouse click on a gun slot shoots the zombie behind it, and on phones your guns sit centered with a reload button by your melee slot. The Barn Buster hits way harder now and reloads faster with every upgrade, and your aim thumb only fires once you push it, so shots go where you mean. And a door that rattles now spits its zombie out right away, no more waiting on the slowpokes, and wave 7 eases up to match. Easy and Normal go way easier: zombies are squishier and slower, and a bite gives you longer to get away. On Hard and Nightmare, Uncle Chunk bulked up: bring your best guns.
- 🤝 Co-op love: a downed buddy now drops to the floor with a glowing ring. Stand in it to pick them up. Game over reads clearly again, with RUN IT BACK for round two. Then the crew board crowns your MVP, Medic, Sharpshooter, and Most Munched, and a tap on the room code copies it. Everyone's rank badge now sits by their name in the lobby and on the crew board.
- 🏠 Munch Manor has a real address now: munchmanor.com. Swing by the steaming mug next to the gear to buy Rattles a coffee.

## October 30, 2026: Bring your friends

- 🧟‍♂️🧟‍♀️ CO-OP is here: up to 4 of you in one manor. Share a room code, or hit QUICK PLAY and we'll find you a crew. Revive your buddies before they bleed out!
- 🎁 Dress up however you like (mohawks, beards, dresses, a rainbow shirt, on any body), unwrap new versions as a rattling gift, and put the game on your Home Screen from the phone tile.
- 🥡 Mystery Leftovers: hold Enter (or the hand button) at the grimy kitchen fridge to pull a Pressure Cooker that lobs exploding lids, a Clang Pan for home runs, or the rare Brain Freeze that turns zombies into popsicles. Take it or leave it! Or a moldy sandwich. Every pull spins like a slot machine and lands with a BOOM, BONK, or BRRR. Rattles will soup them up.
- 🔪 You always pack a knife now, in its own slot right of your guns: tap it (or press F), then fire to stab whatever gets too close. Trade your pistol for a second real gun, and Rattles hands it back for free whenever you miss it.
- 🧹 Playtest fixes: just stand still at a price tag to buy it (no button needed), one big PLAY arrow, the SMG is the Chatterbox, kitchen boards open with a hold, crisper sprites, smoother big hordes on slow phones, truer aim on upright phones, and Hard and Nightmare ease off a hair.

## October 29, 2026: How tough are you?

- 💀 Pick how many skulls right by PLAY, and beat Hard to unlock Nightmare. Waves are tighter, planks cost more, and nothing climbs through a boarded door.
- 💣 Meet the Kaboomer, who brought a bomb to share, and the Big Leaguer, who pitches zombies. Big Lugs wear junk armor now, and point-blank shots always land.
- 🃏 A brand new menu: tap MORE to deal your cards, from the Munchopedia to your own Profile, where you strut in a spotlight, carve your name on a tombstone (Snackula, anyone?), and summon your other devices through a portal. It even plays on a plane.
- 📱 Play holding your phone upright: the manor zooms in up close, and your guns sit on the wall right under your thumbs. Turn it sideways any time to see the whole room.
- 🍲 Smash open the kitchen behind the parlour: shoot the ovens to toast zombies, watch them slip in spilled soup, and last ten waves. The parlour got cozy, Rattles' shop got big pictures and a pricey Laser Sight, and your cursor is big and yellow so the dark floor never swallows it.
