# What's new in Munch Manor

One entry per day, newest first. Only things a player would notice, a handful of bullets at most, written for players and not for us.

## October 31, 2026: Rattles has notes

- 🧭 New here? Your first breaks walk you through it: a free Plank Pile from Rattles, then a free kitchen and a free fridge roll, with the clock on hold. Confetti included.
- 🔫 Your gun now yells before it runs dry: the ammo blinks amber, then red, the last shots tick, and Rattles hollers from his stall, where his AMMO window now sells you more mid-wave. Out of everything? Out comes the knife, or the Clang Pan, which now takes the knife's slot so you keep both guns. On Easy and Normal, your gun reloads itself the moment you ease off the trigger.
- 💀 Getting munched is a moment now: you topple, the zombies pile on, MUNCHED! slams down, and your run counts up. Go further or splat more than ever for a NEW BEST! Winning rains confetti (and a little gore) and crowns the MVP in co-op. Hit SHARE to brag anywhere: it opens your phone's share sheet, or copies your brag to paste. And no more walling the room shut: planks board up half the doors at most.
- 🔍 Phones zoom in closer, so zombies are easier to spot, and your thumb's aim gets a little zombie magnet held sideways too. Taps land where you mean them too: the settings X closes every time, buttons answer with another finger down, a mid-wave mouse click on a gun slot shoots the zombie behind it, and on phones your guns sit centered with a reload button by your melee slot. The Barn Buster hits way harder now and reloads faster with every upgrade, and your aim thumb only fires once you push it, so shots go where you mean. And a door that rattles now spits its zombie out right away, no more waiting on the slowpokes.
- 🤝 Co-op love: a downed buddy now drops to the floor with a glowing ring. Stand in it to pick them up. Game over reads clearly again, with RUN IT BACK for round two.
- 🏠 Munch Manor has a real address now: munchmanor.com. Swing by the steaming mug next to the gear to buy Rattles a coffee.

## October 30, 2026: Bring your friends

- 🧟‍♂️🧟‍♀️ CO-OP is here: up to 4 of you in one manor. Share a room code, or hit QUICK PLAY and we'll find you a crew. Revive your buddies before they bleed out!
- 🎁 Dress up however you like (mohawks, beards, dresses, a rainbow shirt, on any body), unwrap new versions as a rattling gift, and put the game on your Home Screen from the phone tile.
- 🥡 Mystery Leftovers: hold Enter (or the hand button) at the grimy kitchen fridge to pull a Pressure Cooker that lobs exploding lids, a Clang Pan for home runs, or the rare Brain Freeze that turns zombies into popsicles. Take it or leave it! Or a moldy sandwich. Every pull spins like a slot machine and lands with a BOOM, BONK, or BRRR. Rattles will soup them up.
- 🔪 You always pack a knife now, in its own slot right of your guns: tap it (or press F), then fire to stab whatever gets too close. Trade your pistol for a second real gun, and Rattles hands it back for free whenever you miss it.
- 🧹 Playtest fixes: just stand still at a price tag to buy it (no button needed), one big PLAY arrow, the SMG is the Chatterbox, kitchen boards open with a hold, crisper sprites, smoother big hordes on slow phones, truer aim on upright phones, and Hard and Nightmare ease off a hair.

## October 29, 2026: How tough are you?

- 💀 Pick how many skulls right by PLAY, and beat Hard to unlock Nightmare. Waves are tighter, planks cost more, and nothing climbs through a boarded door.
- 💣 Meet the Kaboomer, who brought a bomb to share, and the Big Leaguer, who pitches zombies. Big Lugs wear junk armor now, and point-blank shots always land.
- 🃏 A brand new menu: tap MORE to deal your cards, from the Munchopedia to your own Profile, where you strut in a spotlight, carve your name on a tombstone (Snackula, anyone?), and summon your other devices through a portal. It even plays on a plane.
- 📱 Play holding your phone upright: the manor zooms in up close, and your guns sit on the wall right under your thumbs. Turn it sideways any time to see the whole room.
- 🍲 Smash open the kitchen behind the parlour: shoot the ovens to toast zombies, watch them slip in spilled soup, and last ten waves. The parlour got cozy, Rattles' shop got big pictures and a pricey Laser Sight, and your cursor is big and yellow so the dark floor never swallows it.

## October 28, 2026: Opening night

The doors are open. Bring snacks. Do not become the snack.

- 🧟 Eight waves of zombies, a shop run by a skeleton, and Uncle Chunk waiting at the end.
- 🎨 Pick your look: body, hair, skin, and shirt.
